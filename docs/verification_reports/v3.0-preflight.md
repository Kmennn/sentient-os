## 3. Known Issues

- **Sim vs Real Gap**: Physics in `manipulation_env.py` is very simple (Kinematic). Real dynamics (friction, inertia) are not modeled. Transfer of distilled rules is safer than policy weights.
- **Optimization Speed**: Hill climbing is slow. PPO/SAC recommended for production (v3.2).
